[ 01 ]The need

Sound familiar?

  • Our training is an event, not a behavior change.

    Learners enjoy the day, then revert in the field within weeks.

  • Teams know the product but not each other.

    Brand, access, field, medical and ops work in silos, and handoffs break at launch.

  • Role-play feels artificial.

    Linear scripts don't reflect messy, shifting, real-world stakeholder situations.

  • We can't see what is working.

    Participation is tracked. Capability, decision quality and consequences are not.

  • Facilitators carry too much.

    Consistency varies across cohorts, and every program is rebuilt from scratch.

  • We need it to scale.

    One brand, one TA, one meeting isn't enough. Leaders want something they can build on.

      [ 06 ]Why it works

      Why life-sciences teams run sims

      1. 1

        Practice

        Teams make mistakes safely, before real customers, patients or launch dates are at stake.

      2. 2

      3. 3

        Evidence

        Rubrics, leaderboards and Insights Reports replace attendance counts with behaviors leaders can coach.

      4. 4

        Built to evolve

        A lab can be refreshed for a new brand, therapeutic area, launch phase or market as things change.

      5. 5

        Staying power

        Pre-work, avatars, micro-challenges and coaching prompts carry the effect for months.
